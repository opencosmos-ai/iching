# 厲 — `peril`

**27 times in the judgments and line texts.** The forms are 貞厲 8 ("holding firm: peril"), 厲无咎 4
("peril; no blame"), 有厲 3 ("there is peril"), 厲吉 3 ("peril; auspicious") and 厲終吉 2 ("peril; in
the end auspicious").

**厲 is not on the scale the other five make.** [`method.md`](../method.md) § 4 grouped it with 悔
and 吝 as "a graded scale". The text says otherwise, in two ways:

- **The Wings leave it out.** 繫辭上 ch 2–3 lays out the verdicts as 吉凶, 悔吝 and 无咎, and 厲
  appears nowhere in either part of 繫辭. It is in the 小象傳 13 times, the 彖傳 and 文言 once each.
- **The lines put it beside verdicts it would contradict if it were one.** It stands with 吉 in 6
  lines and with 咎 in 9. In 12 of the 15 lines that have both, it comes first and the verdict
  after: 厲，无咎 (1.3), 厲吉 (27.6), 貞厲，終吉 (6.3). No word on a scale from 吉 to 凶 could stand
  next to 吉.

**So 厲 names the situation and the verdict follows it. It is a condition, not an outcome.** Its
English has to be a condition word that a verdict can follow: "peril; no blame." See
[`WORKLIST-CLOSED.md` § A4](../WORKLIST-CLOSED.md#a4--the-verdict-vocabulary--done).

## The evidence

**The graph.** 說文: 旱石也。从厂，蠆省聲, "a dry stone; from 厂 (a cliff), sounded by a
shortened 蠆." The danger is a later sense. Xu Shen's entry has only the stone.

**The witnesses converge on danger, and split on where it is.**

| | renders 厲 as |
|---|---|
| Legge | *peril* / *perilous*, in 25 of 27 |
| McClatchie | *danger* or *anxiety* in 21 of the 24 legible: the danger outside, or the fear inside |
| de Harlez | *danger* 6 of 9, *difficultés* elsewhere |

## The ruling — `peril`, Shalom's call, 2026-10-20

**The neighbouring words are spoken for.** 險 is *hazard* (Shalom, 2026-09-25), because the Laozi
keeps *danger* for 殆 alone. *Peril* is unused in the Tao Te Ching translation. It is a condition you
are in, so a verdict can follow it, and it is Legge's word in 25 lines out of 27. That convergence is
support, not contamination ([`convergence-is-evidence`](../principles/convergence-is-evidence.md)).
The readings have carried it as the working gloss since 2026-09-25, 78 times.

## What is ruled out, and why

- **"danger"** is 殆's, in the Tao Te Ching (chs 16, 25, 32, 44, 52), and it is why 險 is *hazard*.
  It is on the `forbidden:` list. Neither 殆 nor 危 occurs in the core text or in any 大象傳, so no
  graph in a file that cites this entry needs the word.
- **"anxiety"** (McClatchie, at 6.3, 18.1, 24.3 and more) moves 厲 inside the person. 厲 is the
  situation. The fear belongs to 惕 (1.3 夕惕若, "at evening, wary") and 恤.

## The seam

**At 37.3 the graph's harsher sense is live.** 家人嗃嗃，悔厲吉: the household scolds sharply, and
厲 may be the severity of the scolding rather than a peril. [`divergence-stays-open`](../method.md#6-the-principles)
keeps that open in 37's notes. The same holds at 52.3 厲薰心 ("peril fumes the heart"), where 厲 is
the subject of the sentence.
