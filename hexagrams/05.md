## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **sky** (乾 · yang · vigour). Without: **water** (坎 · yang · sinking in).
**Across** — sky ↔ earth meets water ↔ fire, yang on both sides, no odd line within and the odd line
at the middle without.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖 (*tuàn*): 需，須也，險在前也；剛健而不陷，其義不困窮矣 (*xū, xū
yě, xiǎn zài qián yě; gāng jiàn ér bù xiàn, qí yì bù kùn qióng yǐ*), "需 (*xū*) is waiting: the hazard
is in front; firm and vigorous and not sinking in, by rights it is not cornered." The 大象 (*dà
xiàng*, the great image): 雲上於天，需；君子以飲食宴樂 (*yún shàng yú tiān, xū; jūnzǐ yǐ yǐn shí yàn
lè*), "cloud rises above the sky: 需; the noble one, by it, eats and drinks, feasts and takes
pleasure." The tradition reads strength that waits for the hazard ahead to open, and waits well.

**What the polarity implies.** Three firm lines inside: the self is all vigour. Outside is the pole
that sinks in, its firm line hidden at its centre. Sky rises and water descends, so the two move
toward each other — but the 大象 stops the image at cloud, rain gathered and not yet fallen. The 彖
does something rare: it names both of the signature's actions and negates the outer one, 剛健而不陷
(*gāng jiàn ér bù xiàn*), "firm and vigorous, and not sinking in." That is the whole figure. The
vigour could plunge; it chooses not to take on the other trigram's action. The lines then measure
the distance to the hazard, step by step — 需于郊 (*xū yú jiāo*) at the outskirts, 于沙 (*yú shā*) in
the sand, 于泥 (*yú ní*) in the mud, 于血 (*yú xuè*) in blood — until the fifth, the firm core of the
water itself, where the waiting is 于酒食 (*yú jiǔ shí*), "at wine and food." The structure suggests
the reward is inside the hazard, reached by not rushing it.

Turn the figure over and it is 6: the hazard inside, the vigour outside, the two moving apart. In 5
strength waits on the world and is fed; in 6 a grievance presses on a strength that will not come
down.

**For the name.** **Waiting** — supported: the 彖 glosses 需 by 須 (*xū*, "wait"), and the signature
says why the vigour waits — its action and the outer one run opposite, and it declines to sink. It
deepens the word toward waiting as held strength rather than delay. **Candidates the signature
suggests:** *Biding* — survives every line: "biding at the outskirts … in the mud … at wine and
food." *Standing Off* — from 不陷 (*bù xiàn*, "not sinking in"); fails the lines at 需于酒食 (*xū yú
jiǔ shí*, "— at wine and food"), where nobody stands off from a meal, and at 入于穴 (*rù yú xué*),
"enters the hole."
