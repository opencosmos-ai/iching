## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **sky** (乾 · yang · vigour). Without: **fire** (離 · yin · attaching).
**Across** — sky ↔ earth under water ↔ fire.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖 (*tuàn*) finds the name in the fifth line:
柔得尊位，大中而上下應之 (*róu dé zūn wèi, dà zhōng ér shàng xià yìng zhī*, "the yielding gets the
honoured place, great at the centre, and above and below answer it"). Its integrity is
剛健而文明 (*gāng jiàn ér wén míng*, "firm and vigorous, and patterned with clarity"), and it
應乎天而時行 (*yìng hū tiān ér shí xíng*, "answers the sky and moves with the time"). The 大象 (*dà
xiàng*) sees 火在天上 (*huǒ zài tiān shàng*, "fire above the sky"), and the noble one
以遏惡揚善，順天休命 (*yǐ è è yáng shàn, shùn tiān xiū mìng*, "checks the bad and lifts the good,
going along with the sky's fine mandate").

**What the polarity implies.** 13 turned over. There fire rose into the sky; here it stands on
top of it — the light of noon over everything beneath. The one yielding line has moved from the
heart of the self (13's line 2) to the ruler's place (line 5), and the five firm lines all answer
to it. What the figure has, it has by an open hand: fire owns nothing and lives on what it clings
to, and here it clings to the whole sky. The structure suggests that great having is the owner's
hollowness — the holder at the top is the one line that grips nothing. The lines agree: 匪其彭
(*fěi qí péng*, "not its swelling", 14.4), 厥孚交如，威如 (*jué fú jiāo rú, wēi rú*, "its 孚 — *fú*,
not yet ruled — goes both ways, and commands respect", 14.5), and 公用亨于天子 (*gōng yòng xiǎng yú
tiānzǐ*, "the lord makes an offering to the son of the sky", 14.3): the having is passed upward, and
the top line receives 自天佑之 (*zì tiān yòu zhī*, "from the sky, help for it"). Set against 9, the
other sky with a yielding trigram above: there a single open line at 4 stores and does not rain;
here it sits at 5 and shows everything.

**For the name.** **Great Possession** — strains: the figure has what it has by being open where
the owner sits, and *possession* leans toward a grip. **Candidates the signature suggests:** *Great
Having* — survives: 大車以載 (*dà chē yǐ zài*, "a great cart, loaded", 14.2) is having, not owning;
*Great Harvest* — survives: the loaded cart and the offering in 14.3 are a harvest year's, and the
sun above the sky is its season; *Great Light* — fails the lines: no line lights anything, and the
light is the image of the 大象 (*dà xiàng*, "the great image") alone. The name waits on what 有 (*yǒu*, "have, there is") is here.
