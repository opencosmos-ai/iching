## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **sky** (乾 · yang · vigour). Without: **lake** (兌 · yin · releasing).
**Across** — sky ↔ earth × mountain ↔ lake: a trigram with no odd line under one open only at the top.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖傳: 夬，決也，剛決柔也 (*guài, jué yě, gāng jué róu yě*, "*guài*
is breaking through: the firm breaks through the yielding"), and at once it tempers it — 健而說，決而和
(*jiàn ér yuè, jué ér hé*, "vigour, and releasing; breaking through, and in accord"). It counts the
lines, 柔乘五剛也 (*róu chéng wǔ gāng yě*, "the yielding rides on five firm lines"), and warns off
force: 告自邑，不利即戎，所尚乃窮也 (*gào zì yì, bù lì jí róng, suǒ shàng nǎi qióng yě*, "announce it
from the town; it is not favourable to go straight to arms — what that prizes comes to an end"). The
大象傳: 澤上于天，夬；君子以施祿及下，居德則忌 (*zé shàng yú tiān, guài; jūnzǐ yǐ shī lù jí xià, jū dé
zé jì*, "the lake has risen above the sky: *guài*; the noble one gives out stipends to reach those
below, and holds that sitting on integrity is to be shunned"). The tradition reads a decisive
clearing-out of the last weak line, done openly and by word rather than by arms.

**What the polarity implies.** A trigram with nothing open in it, under a trigram open only at its
lip. All the force of the figure has one exit, and it is at the very top: five firm lines press up
and the sixth gives. The lake raised above the sky is a body of water held up at the height of the
figure, full to the brim, with vigour still rising beneath it. What comes next is not a push but an
overtopping — the break happens at the place already open.

The structure suggests why the 彖傳 insists on 決而和 (*jué ér hé*, "breaking through, and in
accord"), and why the judgement goes to the court and not the field. The outlet is the lake, and the
lake's action is 說 (*yuè*) — release, and also speech. Vigour within, speech without: resolve that
becomes a proclamation, 揚于王庭 (*yáng yú wáng tíng*, "raise it in the ruler's court"). The same
reading accounts for the top line's warning, 无號，終有凶 (*wú háo, zhōng yǒu xiōng*, "no crying out:
in the end, misfortune") — if the one open place does not speak, the force has nowhere to go. Turn
the figure over and it is 44: the open line at the bottom, arriving instead of leaving, and the
strength no longer the self's but the world's.

**For the name.** *Resolve* — the signature supports it and deepens it: resolve here is firmness that
has found its one outlet, and the outlet is a voice. **Candidates the signature suggests:**
*Clearing*, *Breakthrough* — *Clearing* survives: 君子夬夬 (*jūnzǐ guài guài*, "the noble one,
clearing, clearing") and 莧陸夬夬 (*xiàn lù guài guài*, "the purslane, cleared, cleared") both carry
the doubled word; *Breakthrough* — the overtopping the figure draws — fails the lines as a noun: 君子
夬夬 (*jūnzǐ guài guài*) must double as a description of a person, and "the noble one, breakthrough,
breakthrough" does not.
