## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **lake** (兌 · yin · releasing). Without: **earth** (坤 · yin · going along).
**Across** — mountain ↔ lake × sky ↔ earth.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖傳: 剛浸而長 (*gāng jìn ér zhǎng*), "the firm soaks in and grows";
說而順 (*yuè ér shùn*), "releasing, and going along"; and a warning, 至于八月有凶，消不久也 (*zhì yú bā
yuè yǒu xiōng, xiāo bù jiǔ yě*), "come the eighth month there is misfortune: the waning is not long
off." The 大象傳: 澤上有地 (*zé shàng yǒu dì*), "above the lake there is earth," and 君子以教思无窮，
容保民无疆 (*jūnzǐ yǐ jiào sī wú qióng, róng bǎo mín wú jiāng*), "the noble one teaches and thinks
without running out, holds the people and keeps them safe without a border." The tradition reads the
firm advancing, the high bending to the low, and an advance that will turn.

**What the polarity implies.** Both poles are yin, so neither trigram has its one ruler. Yet the
figure's only two firm lines are inside, at the bottom of the lake (`110000`). The lake's strength
is its bed, and it is rising, while the earth above has no firm line at all. So the overseeing here
has an odd shape. The land stands over the water like a bank above a lake, which is 說文's 監臨
(*jiān lín*, "bending to look down"). But the force is in what is looked at, and it is coming up. The
structure suggests that to oversee, in this figure, is to be the shore: wide, yielding and
containing (容, *róng*, "hold") rather than commanding, while the living thing below presses up
through the openness. That also explains 甘臨 (*gān lín*, "sweet overseeing"), which gains nothing.
Ground that only goes along, over water that only releases, is all ease and no edge. Line 3, where
the lake's open top meets the earth, is where that happens. 19 turned over is 20. Here the firm is
gathering at the bottom and arriving; in 20 it has reached the top and is on show. The 雜卦 makes
them one sentence: 臨、觀之義，或與或求 (*lín guān zhī yì, huò yǔ huò qiú*), "the meaning of 臨 and
觀: one gives, one seeks."

**For the name.** **Overseeing**. The signature strains it slightly, because it puts the force
below and advancing, and *overseeing* names only the shore's side. But every line is a mode of the
one above (咸臨, 至臨, 知臨, 敦臨), so the lines hold the name where the figure pulls. **Candidates the
signature suggests:** *Approach* survives the lines (至臨, *zhì lín*, "an arriving approach"; 甘臨,
"a sweet approach"), but it loses the bending-down that 說文 gives. *Rising* fails the lines: 知臨，
大君之宜 (*zhī lín, dà jūn zhī yí*), "a knowing ___, fitting for a great lord," cannot take "a
knowing rising," and 46 升 already has rising.
