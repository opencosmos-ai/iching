## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **fire** (離 · yin · attaching). Without: **sky** (乾 · yang · vigour).
**Across** — water ↔ fire under sky ↔ earth.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖 (*tuàn*) finds the name in one line: 柔得位得中，而應乎乾
(*róu dé wèi dé zhōng, ér yìng hū qián*, "the yielding gets its place and the centre, and answers
乾, the sky"). It reads the two actions as 文明以健 (*wén míng yǐ jiàn*, "pattern and clarity, with
vigour"), and ends 唯君子為能通天下之志 (*wéi jūnzǐ wéi néng tōng tiān xià zhī zhì*, "only the noble
one can get through to the aims of all under the sky"). The 大象 (*dà xiàng*) sees 天與火 (*tiān yǔ
huǒ*, "sky with fire"), and the noble one 以類族辨物 (*yǐ lèi zú biàn wù*, "sorts clans by kind and
tells things apart") — a gathering that still knows what differs.

**What the polarity implies.** Fire rises, and the sky is above: this is a figure whose two
trigrams have the same heading. 11 is two things meeting from opposite sides; 13 is two things
going the same way, which is what 同 (*tóng*, "together, the same") says. Fire attaches — it has
nothing of its own and lives on what it clings to — and its odd line is the hollow at its middle,
line 2, the only yielding line in the figure. A gathering, the structure suggests, needs an empty
centre that others can come into. But attaching clings to what is nearest, and the lines mark that
exactly on fire's own line: 同人于宗，吝 (*tóng rén yú zōng, lìn*, "people together in the clan:
narrowness", 13.2). Left to itself, fire makes a faction. The vigour above is what carries it out,
and the lines travel outward with it — 于門 (*yú mén*, "at the gate", 13.1), through the ambush and
the walls, to 于郊 (*yú jiāo*, "at the outskirts", 13.6), and the judgment's 于野 (*yú yě*, "in the
open country"). Against 14, its figure turned over: here the one open line is at the heart of the
self (line 2); there it sits in the ruler's place (line 5).

**For the name.** **People Together** — supports, and the signature deepens *together* into
*going the same way*, which is why the name holds when the lines turn to armies. **Candidates the
signature suggests:** *Joining People* — survives: 同人于宗，吝 (*tóng rén yú zōng, lìn*) reads as "joining people in the clan:
narrowness", and 大師克相遇 (*dà shī kè xiāng yù*, "the great armies prevail and meet", 13.5) as the
joining of two forces; *Kindred* — fails the lines: the kin gathering 于宗 (*yú zōng*, "in the clan") is the one line marked
吝 (*lìn*, "narrowness"), so a kin word would name the figure by its fault.
