## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **earth** (坤 · yin · going along). Without: **mountain** (艮 · yang · stopping).
**Across** — sky ↔ earth × mountain ↔ lake.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖傳: 剝，剝也，柔變剛也 (*bō, bō yě, róu biàn gāng yě*), "stripping
is stripping: the yielding changes the firm." It reads the trigrams as counsel, 順而止之，觀象也 (*shùn
ér zhǐ zhī, guān xiàng yě*), "going along, and stopping it: watch the image," and it sets the whole in
a cycle: 君子尚消息盈虛，天行也 (*jūnzǐ shàng xiāo xī yíng xū, tiān xíng yě*), "the noble one values
waning and waxing, filling and emptying: that is the sky's going." The 大象傳: 山附地上 (*shān fù dì
shàng*), "the mountain leans on the earth," and 上以厚下，安宅 (*shàng yǐ hòu xià, ān zhái*), "those
above thicken those below and settle the house." The tradition sees the yielding climbing to the last
firm line. The advice is not to set out, to go along and hold, and to thicken the base.

**What the polarity implies.** Five open lines sit under one firm one (`000001`). The earth within
has no firm line at all. The only firm line in the whole figure is the mountain's lid, at the very
top, with nothing above to hold it and nothing below that will. The base does not fight. It gives
way, line by line, and what gives way underneath takes the top with it. The lines name this in
order: the bed stripped at the legs, at the frame, at the skin (剝牀以足 … 以辨 … 以膚, *bō chuáng
yǐ zú … yǐ biàn … yǐ fū*). The structure suggests stripping is what happens to a lid with no
ground under it. But the top still holds one thing: 碩果不食 (*shuò guǒ bù shí*), "the big fruit is
not eaten." 說卦 ch 11 has 艮…為果蓏 (*gèn wéi guǒ luǒ*, "mountain is fruit"), so the last firm line
is a fruit, with a seed in it. The same chapter has 坤…為大輿 (*kūn wéi dà yú*, "earth is the great
cart"), and that answers the rest of line 6: 君子得輿 (*jūnzǐ dé yú*), "the noble one gets a cart."
The earth that stripped the firm line is the earth that will carry it. Turn the figure over and the
fruit has fallen and lies at the bottom: that is 24. The 大象 has already said where to put the work.
厚下, "thicken what is below," is where the firm line is going next.

**For the name.** **Stripping**. The signature supports it. The figure is a lid whose ground gives
way from beneath, and *stripping* keeps the transitive agent the lines need. It is deepened by line
6: stripping reaches a core it does not eat. **Candidates the signature suggests:** *Peeling*
survives the lines (剝牀以膚, "peel the bed to the skin"), and it carries the signature's own fruit,
but it is small and domestic for 剝廬 (*bō lú*, "strip the hut"). *Collapse* fails the lines: it is
intransitive, so "collapse the bed at the legs" does not parse, as `hexagram-names.md` already rules.
