## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **earth** (坤 *kūn* · yin · going along). Without: **fire** (離 *lí* · yin · attaching).
**Across** — the yin pole of sky ↔ earth within, the yin pole of water ↔ fire without.

**What the tradition reads.** Both Wings read a sunrise. The 大象: 明出地上，晉；君子以自昭明德 (*míng chū
dì shàng, jìn; jūnzǐ yǐ zì zhāo míng dé*, "clarity comes out above the earth: progress; the noble one
makes their own clear integrity shine"). The 彖 glosses the name 晉，進也 (*jìn, jìn yě*, "晉 is going
forward") and reads the two actions in order: 順而麗乎大明，柔進而上行 (*shùn ér lì hū dà míng, róu jìn ér
shàng xíng*, "going along, and attaching to great clarity; the yielding goes forward and moves up"). The
tradition takes the figure as advance that is also a coming into view — the lord 晝日三接 (*zhòu rì sān
jiē*, "received three times in a single day").

**What the polarity implies.** Both trigrams are yin, and no firm line anywhere pushes from below: the
earth is all open, and the only firm lines are the fire's two outer ones, framing a hollow centre. So
what rises here is not driven; it is carried. The earth goes along, and on its back the sun clears the
horizon. That is this figure's progress — the dawn, the one ascent in which nothing strains. Fire's
action is attaching: light has no body and must cling to something to be seen, and here what it clings
to is the whole open earth beneath. The structure suggests that to go forward, in 35, is to become
visible — which is why 雜卦 can gloss it with a time of day, 晉晝也 (*jìn zhòu yě*, "progress is
daytime").

The lines ask for patience, not force: 晉如，摧如 (*jìn rú, cuī rú*, "going forward — and pushed back"),
失得勿恤 (*shī dé wù xù*, "loss and gain — do not fret"). And the one firm line that sits low, at the base
of the fire, is the figure's warning: 晉如鼫鼠 (*jìn rú shí shǔ*, "going forward like a rat") — the
grasping thing caught in daylight. Turn the figure over and the same sun is under the earth: 36.
35 is clarity as the world one walks into; 36 is clarity as the self one hides.

**For the name.** **Progress** — the signature supports it and deepens it toward emergence: advance by
degrees, carried and seen, not forced. **Candidates the signature suggests:** *Rising*, *Sunrise* —
*Rising* survives the lines: 晉如，摧如 (*jìn rú, cuī rú*, "rising — and pushed back"), 晉如鼫鼠 (*jìn rú shí
shǔ*, "rising like a rat"), 晉其角 (*jìn qí jiǎo*, "rising on its horns"); *Sunrise*, from 明出地上 (*míng
chū dì shàng*, "clarity comes out above the earth"), fails the lines — 晉如，摧如 cannot be "sunrise-like,
pushed back", and 晉其角 cannot be "sunrise on its horns".
