## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **Lake** (兌 · yin · releasing). Without: **Wind** (巽 · yin · entering).
**Across** — mountain ↔ lake and thunder ↔ wind, both yin.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖 (*tuàn*): 柔在內而剛得中 (*róu zài nèi ér gāng dé zhōng*), "the yielding is inside, and the firm holds the centre"; 說而巽，孚，乃化邦也 (*yuè ér xùn, fú, nǎi huà bāng yě*), "delighting and entering: 孚 (*fú*) — and so it changes a country"; 信及豚魚也 (*xìn jí tún yú yě*), "trust reaches even pigs and fish"; 乘木舟虛也 (*chéng mù zhōu xū yě*), "riding on wood, the boat empty." The 大象 (*dà xiàng*): 澤上有風，中孚；君子以議獄緩死 (*zé shàng yǒu fēng, zhōng fú; jūnzǐ yǐ yì yù huǎn sǐ*), "wind above the lake: 中孚; the noble one deliberates on lawsuits and delays executions." The tradition reads a hollow centre that persuades — as far as the animals, as far as the condemned.

**What the polarity implies.** Both trigrams are yin, and each turns its odd line toward the other: the lake's open line is at its top, the wind's at its base, so the two meet in the middle and make a hollow between firm lines — `110011`. Four figures bring two odd lines together at the centre; this is the only one where both are open. Wind over a lake shows nothing of itself; it shows on the water, which answers. Releasing opens upward, entering opens downward: two openings, facing. That is the lines' own image, 鳴鶴在陰，其子和之 (*míng hè zài yīn, qí zǐ hè zhī*), "a crane calls in the shade; its young answer" — a call unseen, answered anyway. The structure suggests that 孚 is what crosses a hollow with nothing solid to carry it: the answer that comes back because both sides are open. It explains the boat and the court as well — an empty hull floats, and a judge who waits leaves room for the answer to arrive. The figure's failure is the reverse, at the top: 翰音登于天 (*hàn yīn dēng yú tiān*), "a wing-sound climbing to the sky" — a call that rises past anything that could answer it.

**For the name.** *Confidence Within* — the signature strains it: *confidence* names something full, and the figure's centre is empty, so if 孚 goes inward the hollow has to be read as room, not lack. The name is pending on 孚 (A6). **Candidates the signature suggests:** *Brooding Within* — survives: 說文's 卵孚 (*luǎn fú*), "brooding on eggs", makes 有孚攣如 (*yǒu fú luán rú*) "there is brooding, clasped close", and the crane with its young is a brooding scene, though English *brooding* also means moody; *The Answer Within* — fails the lines: 孚 must render 42 times, and 有孚攣如 as "there is an answer, clasped close" does not hold.

## The heart of it

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-25 — an interpretive reading, not a translation. Each claim is marked by
layer: the core text (the judgment and the six lines, Western Zhou), the Wings (Warring States to
Han), 王弼 (*Wáng Bì*, 3rd century), and echoes of the Tao Te Ching in this project's own translation.
The formula words — 吉, 凶, 无咎, 亨, 孚, 貞 — carry working glosses until WORKLIST A4 and A6 rule on
them ([`hexagram-names.md`](../hexagram-names.md)).*

### Essence

**A call from a hidden place, answered — what is held at the centre reaches others without being
sent.** The core text gives the figure one picture and keeps returning to it: a crane calling
unseen, its young answering; a cup offered and shared; a bond 攣 (*luán*, "tied close") at the fifth
line. The name's second graph will not settle, and this reading does not settle it for it. 說文
(*shuōwén*) gives 孚 (*fú*) as 卵孚也 (*luǎn fú yě*, "brooding on eggs") — a hand (爪, *zhǎo*) over a
child (子, *zǐ*) — and then, in the same entry, 一曰信也 (*yī yuē xìn yě*, "some say: 信, *xìn*,
trust"). The dictionary itself holds the fork: a bird covering its clutch, or a word that holds. The
working gloss here is *confidence* (A6); 中孚 (*zhōng fú*) is *Confidence Within* (proposed). Read
either way, it is something kept in the middle that others answer to.

### The polarities in it

The lake within opens at its top; the wind without opens at its base. So the two openings face each
other across the middle of the figure, and `110011` is firm at both rims and open at the heart — the
彖 (*tuàn*)'s 乘木舟虛也 (*chéng mù zhōu xū yě*, "riding on wood, the boat empty"). But the text puts its
good fortune somewhere the hollow does not: at the firm centres of each trigram, lines 2 and 5, the
crane and the bond. The open lines 3 and 4 are where the figure swings and loses its mate. **The
structure gives the room; the lines say what fills it is firm.** Both are true, and the 彖 says both
at once: 柔在內而剛得中 (*róu zài nèi ér gāng dé zhōng*, "the soft is inside, and the firm holds the
centre").

### In nature

Wind cannot be seen on land until something moves; on open water it can. Stand above a lake on a
still morning and watch the dark patches run across the surface ahead of the air you feel — the
water answering the wind before it reaches you. That is the 大象 (*dà xiàng*): 澤上有風 (*zé shàng yǒu
fēng*, "wind above the lake"). Nothing passes from one to the other but a touch, and the whole
surface replies.

The crane is exactly observed. 說文 glosses 鶴 (*hè*) with an old song: 鳴九臯，聲聞于天 (*míng jiǔ gāo,
shēng wén yú tiān*, "it calls in the nine marshes; its voice is heard in the sky"). Cranes keep in
contact by voice in tall reeds where they cannot see each other, and the call of a large crane
carries a long way. 在陰 (*zài yīn*) is "in the shade" — the shaded bank, where the bird is hidden.
The young do not see the parent. They hear it, and answer.

Set that against the sixth line. 翰 (*hàn*) is, in 說文, 天雞赤羽也 (*tiān jī chì yǔ yě*, "the red
plumage of the sky-fowl") — a pheasant-kind of bird. Anyone who has put up a pheasant knows the
sound: a loud burst of wings and a harsh cry, a steep climb, and then a long glide down a field or
two away. It cannot hold height. The crane's voice reaches the sky while the crane stays in the
marsh; the pheasant's sound climbs where the bird cannot follow.

### The movement of the lines

**The judgment.** 中孚：豚魚吉，利涉大川，利貞 (*zhōng fú: tún yú jí, lì shè dà chuān, lì zhēn*) —
"Confidence within: piglet and fish, auspicious. Advantageous to cross the great river;
advantageous to hold firm." 說文 has 豚 (*tún*) as 小豕也 (*xiǎo shǐ yě*, "a small pig"), with a hand
holding meat 以給祠祀 (*yǐ jǐ cí sì*, "to supply offerings") — so the core text may mean the humblest
offering is enough. The 彖 reads it otherwise: 信及豚魚也 (*xìn jí tún yú yě*, "trust reaches even pigs
and fish"). 王弼 follows the 彖: fish are the hidden creatures, pigs the lowliest of beasts.

**Line 1.** 虞吉，有他不燕 (*yú jí, yǒu tā bù yàn*) — "虞: auspicious; if there is another, no ease."
王弼 reads 虞 as 專 (*zhuān*, "single-minded"): one bond, held, is well; a second divides it.
(The graph 燕, *yàn*, "at ease", is 說文's swallow.)

**Line 2** is the heart of the figure. 鳴鶴在陰，其子和之，我有好爵，吾與爾靡之 (*míng hè zài yīn, qí zǐ
hè zhī, wǒ yǒu hǎo jué, wú yǔ ěr mí zhī*, "a crane calls in the shade, its young answer; I have a
fine cup — I will share it out with you"). 爵 (*jué*) is a bird-shaped wine vessel; 說文 says it
takes its shape from a bird whose cry is 節節足足 (*jié jié zú zú*, "measured, measured, enough,
enough"). A firm line at the lower centre: the call and the cup are the same gesture.

**Line 3** meets its match. 得敵，或鼓或罷，或泣或歌 (*dé dí, huò gǔ huò bà, huò qì huò gē*, "meets a
counterpart: now drumming, now stopping, now weeping, now singing"). 敵 (*dí*) is an equal — rival
or match, the text does not say which. An open line out of place at the seam: all feeling, no
footing.

**Line 4** lets go. 月几望，馬匹亡，无咎 (*yuè jī wàng, mǎ pǐ wáng, wú jiù*, "the moon nearly full; the
horse's mate is gone — no blame"). The 小象 (*xiǎo xiàng*): 絕類上也 (*jué lèi shàng yě*, "it cuts
from its kind and goes up") — toward line 5.

**Line 5** is the bond itself. 有孚攣如，无咎 (*yǒu fú luán rú, wú jiù*, "there is confidence, tied
close — no blame"). 說文 gives 攣 as 係也 (*xì yě*, "tied"). On the brooding reading, the same words
say "there is brooding, clasped close".

**Line 6** is the call with nothing behind it. 翰音登于天，貞凶 (*hàn yīn dēng yú tiān, zhēn xiōng*,
"a wing-sound climbing to the sky: holding firm, ominous"). The 小象 asks 何可長也 (*hé kě cháng yě*,
"how could it last?").

### In a life now

This is the figure of a parent's voice in the next room at night; of a teacher whose student
answers years later; of a friendship that does not need to be tended aloud; of the court or the
committee that waits before deciding someone's future. It does not counsel persuasion. It counsels
keeping one thing in the middle (line 1's single-mindedness), and offering what you have plainly —
the shared cup — then letting the answer come. Its warning is at the top: the announcement that
climbs higher than anything it rests on, the public voice that has lost its marsh.

The 大象 turns the image toward the hardest place it could: 君子以議獄緩死 (*jūnzǐ yǐ yì yù huǎn sǐ*,
"the noble one deliberates on lawsuits and delays executions"). 王弼 gives the reason: 信發於中雖過可亮也
(*xìn fā yú zhōng suī guò kě liàng yě*, "when trust comes from the centre, even a fault can be seen
clearly"). Where there is room at the centre, there is time for the answer to arrive.

### An echo from the Laozi

**Shared at the Wings' layer only.** 雜卦 (*záguà*) glosses the name 中孚信也 (*zhōng fú xìn yě*, "中孚
is trust"), and Shalom's chapter 21 ends: *"Its essence utterly real, and within it there is
trust."* The same two graphs, 中 (*zhōng*, "within") and 信 (*xìn*, "trust"), and the same English.
But the core text's word is 孚, not 信, so the echo does not reach the judgment or the lines; and
it cannot, since *trust* is 信's locked word and 孚 must find another. The books also part in kind.
Chapter 21's trust is in the Tao, vague and elusive, before anything answers it. 61's is between
creatures — a call and a reply, a cup and a guest. The Laozi glossary reads 信 as a tally whose
halves match; 61 is closer to that than to chapter 21.

### Words kept

- 中孚 (*zhōng fú*) — *Confidence Within*, proposed; **waits on 孚** (A6)
- 孚 (*fú*) — *confidence*, working gloss; the fork held open: brooding (說文 卵孚也), trust (說文 一曰信也, but *trust* is locked to 信), captive or pledge; never *faith*, never *sincerity*
- 信 (*xìn*) — *trust*, locked; appears here only in the Wings
- 貞 (*zhēn*) — *holding firm*, working gloss; at line 6 the older sense, "the inquiry: ominous", reads as well
- 敵 (*dí*) — *counterpart*: rival or match, left undecided
- 天 (*tiān*) — *the sky*, never *Heaven* (line 6)
- 大川 (*dà chuān*) — *the great river*
