## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **fire** (離 · yin · attaching). Without: **lake** (兌 · yin · releasing).
**Across** — water ↔ fire and mountain ↔ lake, both at their yin poles.

**What the tradition reads.** The 彖傳 sets the two against each other: 水火相息 (*shuǐ huǒ xiāng xī*,
"water and fire put each other out"), and 二女同居，其志不相得 (*èr nǚ tóng jū, qí zhì bù xiāng dé*,
"two women live under one roof, and their wills do not meet"). It ends well only because the change
is trusted — 革而信也 (*gé ér xìn yě*, "changed, and trusted") — and because 文明以說 (*wén míng yǐ yuè*,
"patterned and clear, and so it releases"). The 大象傳 gives the image 澤中有火 (*zé zhōng yǒu huǒ*, "fire
in the middle of the lake") and draws from it 君子以治歷明時 (*jūnzǐ yǐ zhì lì míng shí*, "the noble one
puts the calendar in order and makes the seasons clear"): change as the turning of the year.

**What the polarity implies.** Both trigrams are yin — two firm lines round one open one — so nothing in
this figure strikes, holds or sinks. The work is done by heat that clings to what it touches, under a
surface that is open at the top. The odd line within is at the middle: the fire's hollow centre, heat
working at the core. The odd line without is at the top: the lake's open mouth, where whatever is loosened
comes away. Change starts at the centre and shows at the surface, and the lines climb exactly that way.
The first binds with the raw hide, 鞏用黃牛之革 (*gǒng yòng huáng niú zhī gé*, "bound with the hide of a
yellow ox") — the hide still whole and still useful. By the top the change is all coat: 大人虎變 (*dàrén
hǔ biàn*, "the great person changes as the tiger does"), 君子豹變，小人革面 (*jūnzǐ bào biàn, xiǎorén gé
miàn*, "the noble one changes as the leopard does; the small person changes the face"). The structure
suggests why the leopard is praised and the changed face is not: a coat grows from the inside out; a face
can be changed from the outside only.

**For the name.** **Shedding** — the signature deepens it: heat at the core, loosening at the open
surface, is how a hide sheds, and it keeps the name clear of 改 (*gǎi*, "alter") and 變 (*biàn*,
"transform") in the lines. **Candidates the signature suggests:** *Molting* — survives the coat lines
(大人虎變, "the great person changes as the tiger does"), though there the verb is 變 (*biàn*, "transform"), not 革 (*gé*), and it
bends at 革言三就 (*gé yán sān jiù*, "talk of change comes round three times"), which is no animal's;
*Tanning* — fails the lines: the first line uses the hide already tanned as a binding, and 革言三就 is speech, not
leather.

## The heart of it

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-25 — an interpretive reading, not a translation. Each claim is marked by
layer: the core text (the judgment and the six lines, Western Zhou), the Wings (Warring States to
Han), 王弼 (*Wáng Bì*, 3rd century), and echoes of the Tao Te Ching in this project's own translation.
The formula words — 吉, 凶, 无咎, 亨, 孚, 貞 — carry working glosses until WORKLIST A4 and A6 rule on
them ([`hexagram-names.md`](../hexagram-names.md)).*

### Essence

**The old skin comes away on its own day, and not before.** 說文 (*shuōwén*) gives 革 (*gé*) as
獸皮治去其毛，革更之 (*shòu pí zhì qù qí máo, gé gēng zhī*, "an animal's hide, worked and stripped of
its hair: 革 is renewing it") — the one graph is the hide and the changing of it. The core text uses
it both ways. The first line binds with hide; every line after it changes something. And the
judgment opens on a day: 巳日乃孚 (*sì rì nǎi fú*, "on the *sì* day, then confidence"). Change here is
never a decision made at will. It has a time, it is believed only when that time has come, and once
it is done it is done.

### The polarities in it

Fire within, lake without, and both are yin — two firm lines round one open one — so nothing in this
figure strikes or drives. Heat works at the hollow centre of the lower trigram; the upper is open at
its top edge, where what has loosened comes away (see *The signature*, above). **The change starts at
the core and shows at the surface.** That is the whole difference the last line draws between the
leopard's coat, which grows out from inside the animal, and the changed face, which is put on from
outside. The 彖 (*tuàn*) sees the other side of the pairing: 二女同居，其志不相得 (*èr nǚ tóng jū, qí
zhì bù xiāng dé*, "two women live under one roof, and their wills do not meet") — a strain inside one
house that only a change will settle.

### In nature

Watch an animal moult. In spring a deer's grey winter coat lifts in tufts and the red summer coat
comes through beneath it; a dog leaves its undercoat on every fence post. No animal decides to do
this. The season does it, and the new coat is already growing before the old one lets go. The
tiger's stripes are the plainest lesson in the book: they are marked in the skin itself, so the coat
renews and the pattern stays. 大人虎變，其文炳也 (*dàrén hǔ biàn, qí wén bǐng yě*, "the great person
changes as the tiger does: the pattern is bright", 小象). 說文 gives 炳 (*bǐng*) as 明也 (*míng yě*,
"clear") and the leopard's 蔚 (*wèi*, "dense") as a thick-growing herb — a pattern that has come in
full.

The first line's hide is observed just as truly. Rawhide is laid on wet and shrinks as it dries, so a
lashing of it grips tighter every hour. 鞏 (*gǒng*) is, in 說文, 以韋束也 (*yǐ wéi shù yě*, "to bind
with leather"). A hide in that state is the opposite of a moult: it holds fast, and 小象 says so —
不可以有為也 (*bù kě yǐ yǒu wéi yě*, "it cannot be used to act").

The 大象 (*dà xiàng*) gives the image 澤中有火 (*zé zhōng yǒu huǒ*, "fire in the middle of the lake").
It is not impossible. Marsh gas rises from rotting mud and can burn on the water; in a dry season
the peat under a fen smoulders for weeks beneath a wet surface. Two things that should put each
other out, living in one place. The 彖 says 水火相息 (*shuǐ huǒ xiāng xī*), and 息 (*xī*) cuts both
ways: "put out", and, in 說文, 喘也 (*chuǎn yě*, "to breathe"). 王弼 takes the second: 息者生變之謂也
(*xī zhě shēng biàn zhī wèi yě*, "息 means giving rise to change").

### The movement of the lines

**The judgment.** 革：巳日乃孚，元亨。利貞。悔亡 (*gé: sì rì nǎi fú, yuán hēng. lì zhēn. huǐ wáng*) —
"Shedding *(proposed)*. On the *sì* day, then confidence. From the source, it gets through. Advantage
in holding firm. Regret goes." The graph is 巳 (*sì*), a day in the cycle; 王弼 reads it as 已 (*yǐ*,
"finished") — 即日不孚巳日乃孚 (*jí rì bù fú, yǐ rì nǎi fú*, "on the day itself, no confidence; when
the day is done, confidence"). Either way the counsel is the same: a change is not believed on the day
it is proposed.

**Line 1** is the hide before any change. 鞏用黃牛之革 (*gǒng yòng huáng niú zhī gé*, "bound with the
hide of a yellow ox"). No verdict at all: too early, so hold.

**Line 2**, the centre below, is the day arriving. 巳日乃革之，征吉，无咎 (*sì rì nǎi gé zhī, zhēng jí,
wú jiù*, "on the *sì* day, then change it; going on is auspicious; no blame"). The same words as the
judgment, now as an act.

**Line 3** sits at the seam and is told to wait. 征凶，貞厲，革言三就，有孚 (*zhēng xiōng, zhēn lì, gé yán
sān jiù, yǒu fú*, "going on is ominous; holding firm is peril; talk of change comes round three times,
and there is confidence"). Neither pushing nor refusing works here. The talk has to come round.

**Line 4** crosses the seam, and the change is made. 悔亡，有孚改命，吉 (*huǐ wáng, yǒu fú gǎi mìng, jí*,
"regret goes; there is confidence; the allotted course is altered — auspicious"). The core text's word
is 改 (*gǎi*, "alter"). The famous compound 革命 (*gé mìng*) belongs to the 彖, about the founders of
the Shang and the Zhou, not to this line.

**Line 5**, the centre above, needs no consulting. 大人虎變，未占有孚 (*dàrén hǔ biàn, wèi zhān yǒu fú*,
"the great person changes as the tiger does; before any divining, there is confidence"). The change is
so plainly right that no one has to ask.

**Line 6** is the change complete, and it turns back to stillness. 君子豹變，小人革面，征凶，居貞吉
(*jūnzǐ bào biàn, xiǎorén gé miàn, zhēng xiōng, jū zhēn jí*, "the noble one changes as the leopard
does; small people change the face; going on is ominous; staying and holding firm is auspicious").
Twice the text says going on is ominous — at 3, too soon, and at 6, too late. The auspicious lines are
2, 4 and 6: the day, the altering, the staying.

### In a life now

This is the figure for a change that has to happen and cannot be hurried: a workplace whose way of
doing things has outlived its use, a family rule the children have outgrown, a vocation someone has
stopped believing in, a public institution that must reform. The text counsels timing, not courage.
Do not change what is still binding you usefully (line 1). Let the talk come round more than once
before you act (line 3). When the day comes, change it cleanly (lines 2 and 4). And when it is done,
stop — do not keep pressing a change that has already taken (line 6). Its sharpest observation is the
changed face: people can comply without changing, and a change that has only reached the face has not
happened.

The 大象 turns it to practice: 君子以治歷明時 (*jūnzǐ yǐ zhì lì míng shí*, "the noble one puts the
calendar in order and sees clearly what season it is"). Before changing anything, know what time it
is.

### Words kept

- 革 (*gé*) — *Shedding*, the proposed name; *hide* where the first line uses the graph as leather; not *revolution*, which is the 彖's compound 革命, not this hexagram's word
- 改 · 變 (*gǎi · biàn*) — *alter · change as* (the tiger, the leopard), kept distinct from 革
- 命 (*mìng*) — *the allotted course*, never *fate*, *destiny* or *mandate* (locked)
- 孚 (*fú*) — *confidence*, working gloss (A6); note for A6 that the 彖 glosses it with 信 (*xìn*, "trust"): 巳日乃孚；革而信也
- 大人 · 君子 · 小人 (*dàrén · jūnzǐ · xiǎorén*) — *the great person · the noble one · small people*
- 明 (*míng*) — *see clearly*, never *enlighten* (locked)
- 凶 · 吉 · 无咎 · 悔亡 · 厲 (*xiōng · jí · wú jiù · huǐ wáng · lì*) — *ominous · auspicious · no blame · regret goes · peril*, working glosses (A4)
