## The signature — a reading

*Draft for Shalom, 2026-09-24. This reads the figure through the Wings — the declared lens
([`README.md`](../README.md#the-lens)) — and it is a reading, not a ruling. The name still comes from
the lines ([`read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums`](../principles/read-a-figure-through-its-spectrums.md)).*

**The signature.** Within: **fire** (離 *lí* · yin · attaching). Without: **wind** (巽 *xùn* · yin · entering).
**Across** — the yin pole of water ↔ fire within, the yin pole of thunder ↔ wind without.

**What the tradition reads.** The 大象 sees a hearth: 風自火出，家人；君子以言有物，而行有恆 (*fēng zì huǒ
chū, jiā rén; jūnzǐ yǐ yán yǒu wù, ér xíng yǒu héng*, "wind comes out of fire: the household; the noble
one's words have substance, and conduct has constancy"). The 彖 maps the household onto within and
without — 女正位乎內，男正位乎外 (*nǚ zhèng wèi hū nèi, nán zhèng wèi hū wài*, "the woman's right place is
within, the man's right place is without") — and ends in order: 正家而天下定矣 (*zhèng jiā ér tiānxià dìng
yǐ*, "set the household right, and all under the sky is settled"). For the tradition the household is
the first order, and every wider one follows from it.

**What the polarity implies.** Fire within: a hollow held between two firm lines — an open, warm centre
enclosed by walls. Wind without: open at its base, entering, pervading. Put them together and the
figure is the hearth and its reach — the fire people gather to, and the heat and smoke that pass out
through whatever is open and into everything nearby. The structure suggests the household is defined
twice: by its enclosure, where the fire's firm top line meets the wind, and by its influence, which goes
out softly. The lines hold both: 閑有家 (*xián yǒu jiā*, "bar in the household") at the start, and 富家
(*fù jiā*, "enriching the household") once the wind is reached.

The 大象's 言有物 (*yán yǒu wù*, "words with substance") arrives from the structure: wind carries only
what the fire gave it, so what goes out of a household is as good as its hearth. Both trigrams are yin —
two daughters in 說卦's family — and 卦辭 asks for 利女貞 (*lì nǚ zhēn*, "favourable for a woman's
steadiness"); the figure has no single ruling line in it, and the one-ruler figure it needs, 王假有家
(*wáng jiǎ yǒu jiā*, "the ruler comes to the household"), arrives from outside. Turn it over and the fire
goes outside and looks away: 38. 雜卦: 睽外也，家人內也 (*kuí wài yě, jiā rén nèi yě*, "estrangement is the
outside; the household is the inside").

**For the name.** **The Household** — the signature supports it: a hearth and its reach is a household,
and *household* rather than *family* keeps both the bar of 閑有家 and the wealth of 富家. **Candidates the
signature suggests:** *Kin*, *The Hearth* — *Kin* survives the lines: 家人嗃嗃 (*jiā rén hè hè*, "the kin
shouting sternly"), though it loses the house in 富家 (*fù jiā*, "enriching the household"); *The Hearth*,
from 風自火出 (*fēng zì huǒ chū*, "wind comes out of fire"), fails the lines — 家人嗃嗃 cannot be "the hearth
shouting sternly", and 人 (*rén*, "people") is in the name.
